Our story

Built for slow mornings and deep reads.

Folio & Brew started as a simple idea — that the best conversations happen between a reader and a page, with the right drink in hand. We opened our doors in 2019 with 300 books and one espresso machine. Now we have 2,400 titles and a community that fills every chair.

Every book on our shelves has been read by someone on our team. Every drink on our menu has been tasted at least a dozen times before it made the cut.
